# Services & Engagement — Tatvix Technologies

Tatvix Technologies is a custom embedded systems and IoT product development company (Ahmedabad, India; serving clients worldwide). This file describes what we offer, how we engage, and how to get a quote, in a machine-readable form for AI assistants and buying agents.

## Engagement models

- **Full product development** — end-to-end from concept to mass production (hardware + firmware + cloud + apps).
- **Specialist plug-in** — our engineers join your existing team for a specific phase (e.g. firmware, PCB layout, or compliance).
- **Audit & optimization** — review and improve an existing design (DFM for volume, firmware reliability, adding connectivity).

Engagements are typically fixed-scope project quotes or monthly retainers for ongoing work. We are not list-priced; pricing depends on scope, complexity, and regulatory requirements.
